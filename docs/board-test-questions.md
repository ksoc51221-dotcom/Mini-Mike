# Board test questions (Package B review)

Ask each question in a fresh chat with Learn mode off, then on. A good answer draws one `<board>` and adds at most two sentences of text. Target: 8 of 10 good.

## Economics

1. **Why does a price ceiling below equilibrium cause a shortage?** A good board: ceiling → demand up and supply down (side by side) → Shortage (key) → queues and black markets.
2. **How does an increase in the money supply lead to inflation?** A good board: more money → lower interest rates → more spending → demand exceeds output → prices rise (key).
3. **Compare a monopoly with perfect competition.** A good board: two columns (monopoly, perfect competition) with matching rows for sellers, price setting and profit in the long run; one key node on the main difference.

## Physics

4. **How does a transformer step voltage down?** A good board: AC in primary coil → changing magnetic field in the core → induced voltage in the secondary → fewer turns means lower voltage (key).
5. **Why does a ball thrown upward slow down and come back?** A good board: upward velocity → gravity pulls down at 9.8 m/s² → speed falls to zero at the top (key) → falls back and speeds up.
6. **Explain the steps of nuclear fission in a reactor.** A good board: neutron hits U-235 → nucleus splits → energy and 2-3 neutrons → chain reaction (key) → control rods absorb neutrons.

## Maths

7. **How do I solve a quadratic by completing the square?** A good board: one step per move (divide by a, move c, add (b/2a)², factor the square, square-root both sides); the key node is the factored square.
8. **Why does the chain rule work for a function inside a function?** A good board: x changes → inner u changes by du/dx → outer y changes by dy/du → a key node titled "Chain rule" with the subtitle "dy/dx = dy/du · du/dx".

## General

9. **How does a bill become a law in the UK?** A good board: first reading → second reading → committee → report and third reading → other House → Royal Assent (key). At most 3 new nodes in a step, so this needs grouped steps.
10. **What happens in the water cycle?** A good board: evaporation → condensation into clouds → precipitation → collection, all arrows pointing down; the last caption says the water evaporates again; the key node is condensation or precipitation.
